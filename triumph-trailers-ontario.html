Galvanized, aluminum or painted?

This is the question worth getting right, because it decides how the trailer looks in five winters.

Galvanized steel

Most of the lineup. Hot-dip galvanized rather than painted, so a chip or a scrape does not become the place rust starts. On Ontario roads in February, that is the difference that shows. Heavier than aluminum, and stronger for it — every dump, deckover, equipment and gooseneck trailer is galvanized steel.

Aluminum

Offered on utility trailers. It will not rust at all and it is noticeably lighter, which matters if you are towing behind a half-ton or an SUV rather than a heavy truck. It costs more than the galvanized equivalent in the same size.

Painted

Found on some enclosed cargo trailers. Cheaper up front. Keep an eye on stone chips along the lower panels.

Is there a lot I can walk around?

No — and that is deliberate. We do not rent a yard, which keeps the overhead out of the price. If the model you want is sitting at the manufacturer's yard, we can arrange for you to meet us there and look it over before you commit; weekends are usually fine with a bit of notice.

How soon can I have it?

Many small and medium trailers go out in a day to a week. Larger models can take a few weeks, and up to three months if frames are not ready for assembly. Call for the real answer on a specific model rather than trusting a date on a page.

How does paying work?

A $1,000 deposit by e-transfer holds the trailer for you, and it is non-refundable. The balance is due on delivery and can be e-transfer, certified cheque, bank draft or cash.

Is financing available?

Yes, through a partner. You apply by email or text and they contact you about credit and terms. How financing works.

Do you take trade-ins?

No — new models only. If you need to move an old trailer first, we will sometimes help you advertise it so you can buy new once it sells.

What about warranty?

Every new trailer carries a one-year structural warranty. Ask us about the current terms on the model you are considering before you order.
