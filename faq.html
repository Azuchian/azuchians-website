Questions, Answered

Delivery, lead times, deposits, financing and warranty — the things people call to ask before buying a trailer.

Do you deliver, and what does delivery cost?

Yes - we deliver every trailer to you. We serve all of Southwestern Ontario west of London and St. Thomas, including Sarnia, Leamington, Windsor, Chatham-Kent, Strathroy, Petrolia, Wallaceburg, Forest, Grand Bend and Dresden, out to the US border. Delivery cost varies by trailer and distance, so it is quoted together with your price rather than posted as a flat rate. Call or text 519-550-4584 and we will quote your address.

Do you have a lot I can visit?

We do not run a yard. If the trailer you are interested in is in stock at the manufacturer's yard, we can arrange to meet you there so you can look it over before you commit. Weekends are normally fine if we get a heads-up. Call or text 519-550-4584 to set that up.

How long does it take to get a trailer?

Many small to medium sized trailers are normally ready to ship in a day to a week. Some larger models can be a few weeks up to three months if frames are not yet ready for assembly. Lead times move, so call or text 519-550-4584 for an up-to-date timeline on the specific model you want.

What deposit do you require?

A $1,000 deposit by e-transfer holds the trailer for you. The deposit is non-refundable. The balance is due on delivery and can be paid by e-transfer, certified cheque, bank draft or cash.

How do I pay?

The $1,000 deposit is by e-transfer. The balance is paid on delivery and can be e-transfer, certified cheque, bank draft or cash. All prices on this site are plus HST.

Is financing available?

Yes. Financing is available through a partner. You apply by email or text with your name, address, phone number, email address and the trailer you want, and the financing partner contacts you directly about credit and terms.

Do you take trade-ins?

No. We only sell new models and do not take trade-ins. If you are buying a new trailer, we will sometimes help advertise your used trailer, and you buy the new one once it sells.

Do you sell used trailers?

No - we sell New 2027 Triumph Trailers only.

What warranty comes with the trailer?

Every trailer carries a 1 year structural warranty, a 5 year axle warranty, and a lifetime warranty on lights.

What kinds of trailers do you sell, and what do they cost?

We sell 30+ new Triumph models: utility, dump, car hauler and equipment, deckover, gooseneck and enclosed. Prices run from $3,415 to $63,123 plus HST, and every model listed on this site shows its own price.

How do I order, or ask about a specific trailer?

Call or text 519-550-4584. Texting usually gets a reply within the hour. You can also email isaak.loewen@yahoo.ca. Viewings and delivery are by appointment.
