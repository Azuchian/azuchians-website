Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Azuchian's Triumph Trailers ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through our website, why we collect it, and how it is used. We keep this simple because we collect very little.

Information we collect

We only collect personal information when you choose to give it to us:

  • Contact details you submit. If you use the "Text Us" feature on a trailer page, we ask for your name and your city or address. This information is placed into a text message on your own phone that you choose to send to us. We use it only to identify you and respond to your inquiry about a trailer.
  • Phone calls. If you call the number on our site, we will have your phone number through your call, used only to speak with you about your inquiry.
  • Financing applications. If you use the "Apply for Financing" feature, we ask for your full name, address, phone number, and email address. This information is placed into a text message on your own phone that you choose to send to us. We use it to start your application and we forward it to our independent third-party financing partner, who contacts you about credit and terms. We do not perform credit checks ourselves and we do not ask for banking, SIN, or credit card details on this website.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which trailers are popular, we record basic, anonymous activity such as how many times a trailer page is viewed and how many times the Call, Text, and Share buttons are used. This information is not tied to your name, phone number, or identity — it is simple counts only. We do not build profiles of individual visitors.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and help you with a trailer purchase.
  • To understand which trailers and pages are most popular so we can improve our inventory and website.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing.
  • We do not ask for payment, banking, or credit card information on this website.

Cookies and advertising

Our website itself does not use advertising cookies. If you arrived here by clicking a Google ad, Google may use its own cookies to measure ad performance, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage ad personalization any time through your Google Ad Settings.

Data storage

Anonymous usage counts are stored with a secure third-party database provider (Supabase). Any contact details you send us by text or phone are kept only as long as needed to serve you.

Your choices

Providing your information is always optional — you can simply call us instead. If you would like us to delete any information you have sent us, just ask.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Call 519-550-4584 and we'll be glad to help.
